**My Favorite Series is:**
![Arknights](Arknights_Rise_from_Ember.WEBP)
> Why I chose this series:
1. It’s a brutal, grimdark war drama, not a typical happy anime. The show treats its war grounded and dark. Nobody is safe, actions have real consequences, and it feels more like a serious political/military conflict than a high-energy shonen show.
2. The villains aren't just "evil for the sake of evil." You actually end up understanding why the antagonists (Reunion) are fighting.
3. The boss fights feel huge and cinematic. When a giant, walking-fortress boss like Patriot steps onto the screen, the weight and sound design make every hit feel heavy.
